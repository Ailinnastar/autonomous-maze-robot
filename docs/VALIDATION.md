# Validation and source notes

## Physical robot outcome

Ailinna confirmed that the team completed the maze using a physical robot and identified the supplied `project_text.txt` as the successful version. The portfolio records that team result. Completion time, maze dimensions, and repeat-run statistics have not been independently measured for this publication.

## Published source

`maze_robot/maze_robot.ino` preserves the supplied sketch's control logic, constants, and comments. Import changes are limited to line-ending and trailing-whitespace normalisation. The previous editor-history baseline is retained in Git history.

## Host-side verification

`tests/test_passed_control.cpp` compiles the real sketch with lightweight Arduino and Servo test doubles. It exercises six groups:

1. Five-step infrared sweeps on each channel.
2. Retention of the latest 32 actions after history overflow.
3. Detection of repeated turn sequences, preference switching, and history reset.
4. Wheel commands for two-wall, single-wall, and open-space steering.
5. Right/left selection, recovery preference, and cancelled turns.
6. Dead-end turnaround, long-corridor stopping, and the finished-state latch.

The harness supplies the function declarations normally generated by the Arduino build. It simulates receiver samples and elapsed time; it does not emulate optical sensing, motor dynamics, or electrical wiring. A host compilation is not an AVR-board compilation or a new physical run.

The stop rule is a timed corridor heuristic rather than finish-marker recognition. Thresholds and servo timings are specific to the team's calibration and require checking on another robot.
